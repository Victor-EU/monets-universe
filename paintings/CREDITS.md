# The paintings

All seven canvases are by Claude Monet (1840–1926) and are in the public domain. The files here are 2048-pixel copies
made from the highest-resolution reproductions we could find; the originals are kept out of the repository in `ref/originals/`.

The hour stops that have a painting of their own in the series borrow that painting's colour statistics (a linear-RGB mean and
spread transfer): the haystacks' *Sunset* from *Stacks of Wheat (Sunset, Snow Effect)* (Art Institute of Chicago), *Morning frost*
and *Snow effect* from *Wheatstacks, Snow Effect, Morning* (Getty Center), the poplars' *Pink sunset* from the Tate's *Poplars on
the Epte*, the pond's *Morning green* from the National Gallery's *The Water-Lily Pond*. The other stops are graded from their
own light relative to the painting's hour.
